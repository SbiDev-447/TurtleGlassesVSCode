
## 0.2.5 (2026-10-01)

### Light theme
- Unified beige/sepia background (#f0e9d2), removed hard whites across UI and editor.
- Improved text contrast (UI and editor foregrounds) for readability on warm background.
- Refined token colors with better differentiation (Gruvbox-like): keywords, control flow, operators, functions, types/classes, booleans, numbers, strings, tags/attributes, parameters and properties.
- Tuned list/selection/focus states and status bar badges for better visibility.
